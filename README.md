# Accounting I · Chapter 1 · Study notes

`26-27_HES-SO-GE_ACCOUNTING I`

Study notes for **Accounting I** (Haute école de gestion de Genève, International Business Management, 2026-2027), covering Chapter 1, *Review of accounting fundamentals*. Free for HEG students to use and share.

**Read it online:** https://marcvlv.github.io/heg-accounting-1/
**Download:** the whole document is the single file `index.html`. Save it and open it in any browser; it works offline.

## What is inside

- The chapter's five ideas and the twelve common operations of slide 44, each taught once as the rule, the journal entry, a worked example and the trap.
- Journal entries in the five-column layout used in class: account debited, account credited, working, debit, credit.
- A practice question after every section, folded by default, with the answer folded inside it.
- Exercises 1 to 9 with full solutions. Exercises 1 to 3 follow the official correction; 4 to 9 are the author's own solutions and say so.
- Four self-check drills.
- A foldable table of contents, a light/dark switch, and an English-French glossary built on the Swiss chart of accounts for SMEs, searchable in both languages. Dotted terms in the text switch to French when clicked.

## Sources

Course syllabus, course overview and Chapter 1 slides (HEG Geneva, 2026-2027); Chapter 1 exercises 1 to 9 and the official solutions to exercises 1 to 3; *Plan comptable suisse PME, version abrégée pour les écoles* (Éditions LEP, 2014). Article references are to the Swiss Code of Obligations.
